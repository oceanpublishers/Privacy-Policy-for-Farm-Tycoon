Privacy Policy for Farm Tycoon

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Farm Tycoon ("we", "our", or "the Game") handles your information when you download, install, and play our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

1. Information We Collect

Farm Tycoon does not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number) directly on proprietary servers.

All game progress, inventory, and unlocked items are stored locally on your device via Android private app storage.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Farm Tycoon uses third-party advertising services provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC) to display non-personalized and personalized banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain device-level information in accordance with their privacy policies, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, and system language
  • IP address and approximate location data
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostic data

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please visit: Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

3. Device Permissions

Farm Tycoon requests only the necessary permissions required for smooth game operation:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for loading Google AdMob advertisements and checking network connectivity.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback during interactive in-game events (planting, harvesting, button presses).

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Farm Tycoon is intended for a general audience (ages 13 and above). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

5. Data Security

Any network communication made by integrated SDKs (e.g., Google AdMob) uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) in transit.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We encourage players to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates take effect immediately upon posting.
